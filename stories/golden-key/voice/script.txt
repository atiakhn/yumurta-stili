He told the whole town he buried ten thousand dollars in his backyard. Well, a golden key worth ten thousand dollars. His backyard is a jungle of weeds, rocks and old tree stumps. His wife sighs. "Nobody will ever find anything in there." He puts up a sign. "Dig for the golden key. Five dollars a day." By Saturday, the line goes around the block. Families bring shovels. Kids bring buckets. A man shows up with a metal detector. Another guy rents a tiny digger. People pull out the weeds to see the ground better. They roll away the rocks and rip out the stumps. Weeks go by. Nobody finds the key. And every morning, they pay again. You see, clearing land like that costs a fortune. Farmers pay whole crews to dig out rocks, pull weeds and turn the soil. He got all of it done for free. And the crew paid him. When the last digger gives up, the yard is soft, flat, dark soil. He plants one bag of pumpkin seeds. By autumn, it's the biggest pumpkin patch in town. And who buys them? The same diggers. Next spring, he loses another golden key. In the field next door. He just bought it.
