He ate at the worst restaurant in town every day for ten years. The soup is cold, the bread is hard, and the waiter sneezes on everything. Across the street is the best restaurant in town, with white tablecloths and a line out the door. His friends beg him. "Just cross the street!" He never does. Same table, same cold soup, every single day. And if the soup ever comes out warm, he sends it back. One night, a new cook tries to fix the place. Fresh bread, hot soup, a little parsley on top. The next morning, that cook is fired. By who? By him. He owns the worst restaurant in town. He also owns the best one, right across the street. You see, every couple who walks past that dump suddenly thinks the fancy place is worth fifty bucks a plate. The worse this one gets, the better that one looks. So, for ten years, he sits there every day, making sure it stays terrible. Then, a famous food critic walks into the dump by mistake. Next morning, the headline: "So bad, it's genius. Five stars." Now the line is on the wrong side of the street.
